How to Use

Quick steps so you can build lyrics for Suno without guessing what goes where.
1
Pick your path
Use a preset, hit Randomize All, build it section by section, or type your own lyrics in the last section.
2
Choose your mode
Lyrics gives writing prompts, Concept gives song ideas, and Suno gives title + style + full lyrics.
3
Generate output
Click Generate Output, then use the tabs in the Output Panel to copy exactly what you need.
4
Paste into Suno
In Suno Advanced mode, paste Full Lyrics into Lyrics and Style Prompt into Styles. Keep Instrumental off.
